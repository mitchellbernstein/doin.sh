# doinMORE and doinWITH

Catalog prices are USD $4.99/month or $49.99/year for doinMORE, and USD $99 per seat/year for doinWITH. doinMORE covers managed sync and hosted integrations; doinWITH covers team collaboration and the internal commercial-use grant described in [commercial license](commercial-license.md). Manual personal use, user-supplied AI, local dictation and local MCP remain free. Model usage is supplied by the user; these prices do not include hosted inference. The catalog and checkout currently use Stripe test mode. Do not advertise either as a live purchasable offer until production activation and acceptance are complete. Existing legacy doinMORE subscriptions retain their price.

MORE and WITH are separate subscriptions, workspaces and entitlements. They do not share customer records, credits or data automatically. A customer switching a personal MORE interval receives a short-lived server quote and must confirm its exact amount; an incomplete payment does not change the price. Existing canceled renewals must be resumed explicitly before changing interval. Team owners can manage seats, renewal cancellation/resumption, failed-payment recovery and receipt export. Payment-method changes use Stripe's customer portal. A personal subscription is not silently replaced when creating a team: the team owner must explicitly stop its renewal, and the already-paid term remains available. No cross-plan credit is implemented.

Before production activation:

- Create and verify live MORE and WITH prices; configure live API and webhook signing secrets.
- Register `https://sync.doin.sh/webhooks/stripe` for `checkout.session.completed` and `customer.subscription.created`, `customer.subscription.updated` and `customer.subscription.deleted`.
- Apply pending D1 migrations remotely and deploy the Worker. The remote listing reports 0005–0009 pending; a further billing migration is planned, with its final filename pending.
- Publish the commercial terms; verify receipt signing-key consistency and self-host receipt instructions.
- Configure Stripe tax registrations/behavior, invoices and receipts, customer portal features, and failed-payment retry/recovery settings. Then verify `/v1/plan`, account portal, quote confirmation, subscription switching, seat changes, cancellation/resumption, recovery and receipt issuance against the deployed service without a real charge.

Failure scenarios include lost create responses, duplicate submissions, quote expiry, concurrent interval changes, payment failure, canceled renewal switching, incomplete/prorated seat updates, pending seat reductions, webhook retries, invalid receipts and old entitlements. Focused local reproduction: `cd cloud && node tests/e2e.mjs` and `node tests/team-e2e.mjs`; expected receipts are `cloud/artifacts/e2e.json` and `cloud/artifacts/team-e2e/report.json`. Native/browser acceptance has separate receipts and must be reported separately.
