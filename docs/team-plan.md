Personal doinMORE remains $4.99/month or $49.99/year. Manual personal local use, user-supplied AI, local voice and local MCP remain free. Managed sync and hosted integrations require paid access.

The owner confirmed deployment policy: **anyone can self-host**. Personal, noncommercial self-hosting is free under the Personal Use License. ## Choose one small team model

| Shape | Benefit | Cost / risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One account subscription per person | Reuses personal billing | Fragmented payment and offboarding; no clear owner for shared data. |
| Team subscription with active-member quantity | Automatic member pricing | Every join/leave crosses billing and membership; payment failure can leave access and quantity inconsistent. |
| Team subscription with purchased seat capacity | One annual invoice, reusable seats, clear payment boundary | Must distinguish paid capacity, occupied seats and pending increases. |

Recommend the third shape for both small and large teams. Separate prices only if the eventual small-team offer requires them; avoid separate implementations. A team starts with one shared Markdown workspace. Add multiple workspaces and granular workspace permissions only after demand is clear. Paid team membership supplies commercial use and team features through separate commercial terms; the team can choose doin-operated hosting or its own deployment. It does not implicitly buy personal cloud storage for each member. Personal and team subscriptions can coexist without automatic conversion or cancellation.

Keep deployment location separate from license scope: `hosted` versus `self_hosted` changes infrastructure responsibility, not whether business use requires payment. A proposed self-hosted license receipt records the licensed organization, seat capacity and term with a Studio Yeehaw signature. It must not contain payment credentials or task contents. Its issuance and verification use Ed25519 signatures with externally trusted public keys. The receipt lasts through its paid annual term and does not promise immediate offline revocation. No mandatory cloud task connection is added merely to validate a receipt. Self-hosted enforcement is implemented locally; its expanded runtime fixture remains pending. Payment secrets and license-signing private keys stay outside the public repo and downloads. Owners run their own email, integration credentials and infrastructure on self-hosted deployments. Local free MCP needs no receipt for personal use.

## Domain and ownership

Existing `accounts`, device sessions, personal `documents`, `subscriptions` and `checkouts` are account-owned. Reuse email magic-link sign-in and device sessions; do not repurpose a personal document or its customer as a shared tenant.

| Proposed object | Ownership and constraint |
| --- | --- |
| Team | Random ID, name, billing customer/subscription IDs, lifecycle state and revision. Team billing has its own customer and mutation lease. |
| Membership | Unique `(team_id, account_id)`, role `owner`, `admin` or `member`, state and timestamps. Exactly one owner initially. |
| Invitation | Team ID, normalized verified email, offered role, token hash, inviter, expiry and single-use claim. Never an account credential. |
| Team document | Team ID, workspace ID, full Markdown, increasing revision and update time. No personal account ID as ownership shortcut. |
| Seat operation | Team ID, stable operation/idempotency key, requested capacity, previous capacity, provider result and reconciliation state. |
| Audit event | Team ID, actor account ID, operation, target ID, time and resulting revision; exclude credentials, invitation tokens and task bodies. |

Owner manages billing, ownership transfer and team deletion. Owner/admin invites and removes members and edits team settings. Members read and update the shared document. Only the owner grants admin rights; invitations cannot grant ownership. Ownership transfer atomically checks the target is an active member. The last owner cannot leave, be removed, or delete their personal account until they transfer ownership or explicitly delete the team. Personal account deletion removes other memberships but must not cascade-delete team documents.

Every request checks the authenticated account's current membership, team lifecycle, role and paid entitlement on the server. Team IDs, document IDs and MCP grants supplied by a client are selectors, never authorization. Each conditional mutation rechecks membership in the same database operation; a previously accepted read does not authorize a later write. Integration credentials are scoped to the team and granted tools, not a departing member's device token. Revocation is checked on each hosted MCP operation. A member cannot export another tenant by changing an ID.

## Seat and payment rules

Use one server-allowlisted Stripe licensed recurring price: `unit_amount=9900`, `currency=usd`, `interval=year`, `interval_count=1`; subscription quantity is purchased seats. The owner occupies a seat. Invitations consume no seats and charge nothing. Accepting an invitation requires the same verified email, an unexpired unused token and available paid capacity. Claiming the last seat and consuming the invitation is atomic; competing claims cannot oversubscribe.

Removing a member frees their seat immediately. The already-paid seat can be reassigned until the annual period ends, unless the owner requested a lower renewal seat count; in that case new joins cannot exceed that reserved renewal capacity until the owner restores it. Proposed policy: no automatic midyear refunds; reductions take effect at renewal. Show occupied seats, paid seats, next-renewal seats, renewal date and annual total separately. Never silently charge for an invitation or activate a member with unpaid capacity.

When more seats are needed, the owner requests capacity, sees an invoice preview and explicitly accepts its amount. Preserve the annual renewal date. Charge the remaining-year prorated increase immediately, using `proration_behavior=always_invoice` with `payment_behavior=pending_if_incomplete` where supported. Keep old capacity until Stripe confirms the update was paid and applied. Unsupported payment methods fail with a clear recovery path; do not fall back to granting unpaid seats. Verify these fields against the pinned API version before implementation. This is an annual subscription with a prorated seat adjustment, not a monthly offer. [Stripe quantities](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/quantities), [proration preview and controls](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/prorations), [pending updates and supported payment methods](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/pending-updates).

Persist a team billing operation before contacting Stripe. Retries use its stable idempotency key; a lost response or delayed webhook triggers reconciliation before another change. Serialize capacity changes, renewal reductions, cancellation and deletion under the team lease. Webhooks are signed, deduplicated and reconciled against current provider state; stale events never grant capacity. Renewal reduction updates Stripe quantity immediately with no proration, while preserving already-paid capacity through the current term. Joining cannot exceed the lower next-renewal capacity. Pending billing changes block another adjustment until reconciled or explicitly reset. The E2E scenario verifies these separate current and renewal quantities. Members beyond the requested renewal capacity must be selected for removal before the reduction is accepted; never remove random users.

Cancellation preserves access through the paid period. At expiry or failed renewal, block shared writes and hosted integrations on the doin-operated service. Retain authorized read/export for recovery; membership revocation still denies access regardless of billing. No grace-period promise until a policy is chosen. Personal local files and free personal local MCP continue to work. A lapsed commercial license does not become free organizational use, even when self-hosted. Preserve user data; expiry/offline license enforcement and a limited export transition require explicit terms before implementation. Taxes, invoice payment terms, seat minimums and refund terms must be disclosed before live team billing.

## Terminal and Markdown

Native commands: `/team create`, `/team switch`, `/team members`, `/team invite <email>`, `/team remove <member>`, `/team transfer <member>`, `/team billing`, `/team seats <count>`, `/team export` and `/team leave`. Keep account and team administration in the TUI. Browsers handle email confirmation, provider consent and Stripe checkout/payment recovery only.

Team workspace selection maps `(team_id, workspace_id)` to a user-chosen local folder in private configuration. Selecting a team or cloud folder activates its separately mapped local workspace for ordinary terminal task edits. New mappings default to a private cache beneath the configuration directory; `/team folder <path>` replaces that mapping explicitly. `/team personal` restores the personal workspace. Each cloud folder retains its own revision and baseline. Personal and team folders must not overlap, including any part of the personal library. Markdown stays portable and contains tasks/status/reminder metadata, never credentials or membership authority. Local MCP serves only its explicitly configured folder; hosted MCP serves only its granted team workspace. Joining a team cannot make arbitrary local files available remotely.

Reuse explicit pull/push with whole-document revision comparison. A stale push returns conflict and leaves both local edits and the cloud document intact. The terminal rejects stale revisions and preserves local edits; the user merges Markdown and explicitly retries. No automatic task upload, background sync or live collaborative editor in the MVP. D1 conditional statements and transactional batches fit the current Worker; no Durable Object is needed for this flow. [D1 database API and batches](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/worker-api/d1-database/).

Offboarding revokes cloud reads/writes, integration calls and pending invitations immediately. Previously downloaded Markdown or exported copies cannot be remotely erased. Show this limitation before a workspace is shared. Integration connections that depend on the departing user's third-party authorization must be revoked or explicitly reassigned through fresh provider consent.

## Failure census and required E2E evidence

Write realistic scenarios before implementation. Run against workerd/D1, a Stripe test adapter and the real terminal client; save request transcripts, invoice/quantity receipts, local Markdown before/after, revision outcomes and process cleanup. No live charges or actual external invitations in automated tests.

1. **Three users, two devices:** owner pays for three seats, admin/member accept email-bound invitations, member edits on two devices while admin changes the document. Stale push conflicts; explicit merge preserves both edits. A fourth user in another team cannot read, export, mutate or call hosted tools through forged IDs.
2. **Invitation and ownership races:** replay a consumed token, wrong verified email, expiry, simultaneous claims on the last free seat, removal during a write, two ownership transfers, owner leave/account deletion. Only one seat claim wins; every remaining team retains an owner; revoked writes fail.
3. **Capacity payment failures:** owner adds two seats midyear, payment fails or requires action, response is lost, worker restarts, webhook duplicates/reorders, paid update arrives late, competing admin invites occur. No new access before paid capacity; retries produce one adjustment; reconciliation neither double-charges nor loses purchased seats.
4. **Offline departure and renewal:** removed user retains an offline copy and attempts push/MCP on reconnect; access is denied. Owner reuses the freed seat, schedules a smaller renewal quantity, then edits the request near renewal while another billing action is pending. Selected removals and billed renewal quantity agree; no random eviction or unexpected refund.
5. **Tenant lifecycle:** renewal failure, cancellation/resumption, export after expiry, team deletion during checkout/payment and owner personal-account deletion. No orphan payable checkout, no deleted team restored by a webhook, no personal document deleted by team operations, no expired or cross-team integration grant honored.

## Larger enterprises and open product decisions

Keep SAML/OIDC SSO, SCIM provisioning, domain verification, retention controls, searchable audit export, legal holds, procurement/invoice contracts and granular workspace roles outside the first release. Validate customer needs before building them. Cloudflare hosting alone does not establish SOC 2, HIPAA or any other compliance promise.

The Personal Use License now expressly permits personal self-hosting and requires a paid commercial license for company/team self-hosting or team features. A paid team offer still needs separate Studio Yeehaw LLC terms granting licensed members internal business use and self-hosting rights within their agreed scope. Paying for a currently available hosted personal subscription alone does not supply those rights. Preserve the restriction on selling or commercially redistributing copies/modifications. The [commercial license implementation proposal](commercial-license.md) identifies the remaining agreement and purchase-flow work; neither document issues commercial rights or activates a commercial purchase.

Defaults implemented locally: the same $99/year seat price for every team, one-seat minimum, prepaid reusable seats, explicit prorated additions, no automatic midterm refunds, renewal capacity reservations, scoped shared folders and offline receipts valid through the paid term. Remaining decisions: production billing activation, enterprise-specific terms and retention/deletion policy. No deployment or live purchase is claimed by this document.

## Self-hosted commercial enforcement

`SELF_HOST_MODE="commercial"` verifies a signed live receipt against separately trusted `LICENSE_PUBLIC_KEY` and `LICENSE_KEY_ID`. `SELF_HOST_OWNER_EMAIL` fixes the verified account allowed to bootstrap the licensed organization. Creation uses the receipt's fixed team ID and exact legal entity, while paid capacity and expiry come from the signed claim. Organizational writes and hosted MCP operations recheck the license; no Stripe connection or official signing secret is needed on the self-hosted server. Billing modifications remain on the official service. Personal mode categorically rejects team operations. Existing authorized folder read/export remains available for recovery after expiry; revocation and tenant isolation still apply.

The expanded fixture adds signed commercial self-hosting and the actual exported Worker's personal-mode owner/billing/document/team boundaries. All nine groups passed after the latest backend changes, including migrations through 0008 for the actual Worker. Native workspace activation and real CLI editing/assignment scenarios also passed their coordinated build and three E2E groups.

## Team assignees

`/assign TASK [MEMBER]` is available only in a team workspace. The picker lists current members able to read the selected shared folder; names and emails are display labels, while portable Markdown stores the canonical account ID. Member properties carry a bounded cached display name, never authentication authority. A stable task marker keeps assignment history attached to the same task across ordinary edits and reorders. A missing current member displays as former-member history and cannot acquire a new assignment.

The shared document writer checks newly assigned account IDs against current membership, account state and inherited folder permissions inside the same conditional revision update. HTTP and hosted MCP use that writer. Read-only actors, expired team access, cross-organization assignees, departed/inaccessible members and stale revisions cannot apply new assignments. Existing assignment history can remain after offboarding. Local edits use the ordinary proposal/confirmation/undo path; they reach the server only through explicit team push.

Nine backend E2E groups passed including scoped three-member assignment, read-only rejection, inaccessible/foreign accounts, history preservation, and a grant removal injected between preflight read and the actual D1 conditional update. The latest run also passed after metadata cache bounds/parser alignment and an expiry-at-commit race were added. Native assignment integration passed the fresh build and real CLI E2E, including canonical IDs, cached names, foreign-member rejection and personal-mode denial before network.
